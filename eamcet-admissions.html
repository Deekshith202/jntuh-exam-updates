🎓 TG EAPCET (TS EAMCET) Counselling 2026

Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) has officially announced the TG EAPCET Counselling 2026 schedule.

Event Dates
Registration, Fee Payment & Slot Booking June 19 to June 28, 2026
Certificate Verification June 22 to June 29, 2026
Web Options Entry June 25 to July 1, 2026
Freezing of Web Options July 1, 2026
Mock Seat Allotment On or before July 4, 2026
Modification of Options July 5 to July 7, 2026
Final Freezing of Options July 7, 2026
Phase 1 Seat Allotment Result On or before July 10, 2026
Tuition Fee Payment & Self Reporting July 10 to July 14, 2026
